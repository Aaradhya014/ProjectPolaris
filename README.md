# 🌍 Polaris

> **Guiding families toward a more sustainable future.**

Polaris is an interactive sustainability platform designed to help families understand how their everyday choices contribute to their environmental impact — and take practical steps to reduce it.

## 🌱 The Problem

Many people want to live more sustainably, but environmental impact can feel abstract and difficult to understand.

Everyday actions such as wasting food, using water, consuming electricity, throwing away plastic, using transportation, and flying all contribute to a household's environmental footprint.

Polaris turns these everyday choices into understandable data and actionable challenges.

## 💡 What Polaris Does

Polaris allows users to:

- 🌍 Estimate their household carbon footprint
- 📊 See which activities contribute most to their impact
- 💧 Track water waste
- 🍎 Track food waste
- ♻️ Track plastic waste
- ⚡ Understand the impact of energy consumption
- 🚗 Consider transportation emissions
- ✈️ Account for flight-related emissions
- 🎯 Take part in sustainability challenges
- 🏆 Earn eco badges for completing challenges

The goal isn't to make users feel guilty about their impact.

**It's to help them understand it and make one better choice at a time.**

## 🧭 How It Works

1. Users enter information about their household's everyday resource use.
2. Polaris processes the information using environmental emission factors.
3. The platform estimates the user's environmental impact.
4. Results are presented in an understandable format.
5. Users can identify their biggest contributors.
6. Polaris provides sustainability challenges that encourage practical action.

## 🌱 Sustainability Challenge

Polaris includes a challenge system that gives users practical actions they can complete over time.

Examples include:

- ⚡ Energy-saving actions
- 💧 Water conservation
- 🍎 Reducing food waste
- ♻️ Reducing plastic waste
- 🚲 Sustainable transportation
- ✈️ Reducing travel-related impact

Users can earn eco badges as they progress through the challenges.
